# 04-foxhole-wiki.md

# Foxhole Wiki Expansion Project

## Project Goal

Build the most useful Foxhole strategy resource on the internet.

Do not build a database.

Do not generate pages that only contain statistics.

The purpose of this wiki is to help players:

- make better decisions
- understand war systems
- avoid common mistakes
- contribute effectively to their faction

Google already understands game data.

Google does not understand player judgment.

This wiki should provide player judgment.

---

# Core Philosophy

Foxhole is not primarily a combat game.

Foxhole is a logistics and coordination game disguised as a war game.

Most new players fail because they focus entirely on combat.

Content should repeatedly explain:

- logistics importance
- resource chains
- coordination
- planning
- faction cooperation

# Writing Style

Write like a veteran player.

Not like a database.

Not like patch notes.

Not like AI.

Every page should feel like:

"A player with 1000+ hours is helping a new player."

# Required Article Structure

Every article should contain:

# Content Generation Rules

Every page must answer:

What should a player actually do?

Why should they do it?

When should they do it?

What mistakes should they avoid?

What do veteran players recommend?

---

# Avoid

Do not generate pages that only contain:

weapon stats

vehicle stats

production numbers

resource tables

recipe lists

These pages are low-value.

They should support strategy content, not replace it.

---

# Internal Linking

Every page should link to:

3-5 related articles.

Example:

Tank Guide

Links to:

Tank Crews

Vehicle Recovery

Logistics Guide

Frontline Support

Armor Tactics

This improves indexing.
